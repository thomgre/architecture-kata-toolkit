## Guidelines

* Ask questions about requirements
* Discuss technology that could work
* Sketch out rough vision
* Discuss / improve
* Prepare answering critical questions

## Rules

* Co-workers not in same group
* (Physical) distance from other teams
* Put laptop aside when discussing and use "real world" tools such as whiteboards, notes, organizing thoughts, flip
charts, post-its to facilitate collaboration. Assign a dedicated note taker sitting behind a laptop.
* Rotate leader/designing role to encourage the sharing of all individual insights and viewpoints
